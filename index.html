Styled In Colour

STYLED IN COLOUR

Colour as
structure.

An editorial study of colour in dress — not basic colour matching, but colour as a visual system. The trilogy moves through Harmony → Opposition → Restraint, showing how colour can create balance, tension and authority.

HARMONYControlled relationships that feel considered rather than predictable.
OPPOSITIONBold contrast used to create energy, tension and visual presence.
RESTRAINTTonal dressing where proportion, texture and silhouette carry the expression.
