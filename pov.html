THE THINKING BEHIND THE IMAGE

What is the image
trying to say?

For RazielAnn, fashion imagery is part of the creative language. The goal is not simply to show a look, but to give the look a world, a mood and a reason to exist.

Point of View asks the questions that sit behind the finished frame: What should the eye notice first? Where does the colour create tension? Does the silhouette lead the story? How does the setting change the meaning of the garment?

This is where styling becomes direction. A bag, a shoe, a colour accent, a pose or an architectural background can shift the entire reading of an outfit.
